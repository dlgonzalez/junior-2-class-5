📐

Grammar: The Present Passive

30 min
The crown is made of gold.
↓
SUBJECT

The object we talk about.

↓
BE

is / are / am

↓
PARTICIPLE

made, worn, used, called

↓
EXTRA

of gold, by princesses…

📐 FORMULA:

SUBJECT + BE + PAST PARTICIPLE + EXTRA INFO

➡️ Do you notice the pattern? Let's compare it with the Active Voice and look at more examples so you can see how it works.
🔍 Active vs Passive. In Active Voice, the person is important. In Passive Voice, the action and the object are important. Look at the differences!
📊

Rules & Examples

Active Voice:

People make these shoes in Italy.

Princesses wear silk dresses.

Passive Voice:

These shoes are made in Italy.

Silk dresses are worn by princesses.

SubjectBePast ParticipleExtra
Itiscalledsilk.
Theyaremade ofleather.
Itisused tomake dresses.
Theyareworn byprincesses.
Cottonisgrownin Colombia.

Rule 1:

Passive = be (am/is/are) + past participle.

Rule 2:

Use it when the action is more important than the person.

Describing a Process: How Shoes Are Made

🐄
Step 1: Leather is collected from farms.

Step 3: The leather ________ (dye) with different colours.

Step 4: The leather ________ (cut) into shapes.

Step 6: The shoes are finished and checked.
📦
Step 7: The shoes ________ (pack) into boxes.
✅ The shoes are packed into boxes.
🚚
Step 8: The boxes ________ (send) to shops.
✅ The boxes are sent to shops.
🛍️
Step 9: The shoes are sold to customers.

Poster Instructions

20 min

📋 Your Task

  1. Draw a traditional outfit from Colombia (or your region).
  2. Write the history (2-3 sentences): Where does it come from? When is it worn? Why is it important?
  3. Describe it (5-6 sentences) using the Present Passive: What is it made of? Who wears it? What is it used for? What colors?
  4. Draw a new outfit for your region (3-4 sentences): Materials, who wears it, why you like it.
  5. Present your poster to another pair or to the class.

💬 Help Phrases:

Traditional:

"It is made of…", "It is worn by…", "It is used for…", "The colors are…", "It has…", "People wear it in…"

New Outfit:

"It is made of…", "It is worn by…", "We designed it for…", "We like it because…"

Presenting:

"Our outfit is called…", "It is made of…", "We like it because…", "Thank you for listening!"
